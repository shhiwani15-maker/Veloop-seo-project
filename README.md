# VELoop Rewards — SEO Growth & Organic Traffic Project

**SEO Internship Deliverable Package** · Prepared 04–18 September 2026 · Submission deadline 28 September 2026

This repository contains the complete SEO implementation package for VELoop Rewards, covering technical audit, keyword research, competitor analysis, content strategy, on-page recommendations, internal linking, and a 90-day growth roadmap — produced entirely from public research, with no CMS, hosting, GitHub, Search Console, or Analytics access used or required.

---

## Project

VELoop Rewards is a rewards and engagement platform (velooprewards.in) where users earn points through features like Watch Ads & Earn, Refer & Earn, Daily Rewards, Spin & Win, Games, Tasks, and more. This project builds a practical SEO growth strategy to generate relevant organic traffic, new registrations, and brand awareness — without relying on paid advertising.

## About VELoop Rewards

VELoop Rewards offers a broad, multi-feature earning model: Watch Ads & Earn, Refer & Earn, Daily Rewards, Daily Streak, Spin & Win, Games, Mining, Tap & Earn, Giveaways, Tasks, Gems, Tokens, VEs, SVEs, an Exchange Center, Wallet, Withdrawals, Leaderboards, Levels, and Badges. The site was audited at its public URL only; see `01-SEO-AUDIT/` for full findings.

## SEO Objective

Answer one question: **how can VELoop Rewards acquire more relevant users from Google and other search engines without relying primarily on paid advertising?** Every deliverable in this repo works toward the funnel: Search → Content → Website → Trust → Registration.

## Research Methodology

All research was conducted using public information only — direct checks of the live site, Google search-visibility checks, and publicly available competitor data. No paid tools, gated data, or competitor account access were used. Where a figure couldn't be verified, it is explicitly flagged for confirmation rather than invented.
